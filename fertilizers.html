What are Fertilizers?

Fertilizers are substances that provide essential nutrients to plants and support healthy crop growth.

Types of Fertilizers

Organic Fertilizers

Made from natural materials such as compost, farmyard manure and plant waste.

Inorganic Fertilizers

Manufactured fertilizers that supply nutrients such as nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium.

Safe Usage

Use fertilizers according to soil test results and recommended agricultural guidance.
