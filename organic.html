What is Organic Farming?

Organic farming is a method of agriculture that uses natural materials and practices to grow crops. It focuses on maintaining soil health, protecting the environment and producing healthy food.

Types of Natural Fertilizers

🌿 Compost

Compost is made from decomposed plant waste and kitchen waste. It improves soil structure and adds nutrients.

🪱 Vermicompost

Vermicompost is prepared using earthworms to convert organic waste into nutrient-rich manure.

🐄 Farmyard Manure

Farmyard manure is prepared from decomposed cattle dung and other farm waste.

Natural Pest Control Methods

🌼 Neem-Based Products

Neem-based products are used in some farming practices to help manage pests. Use approved products according to their labels.

🌾 Crop Rotation

Growing different crops in sequence can help reduce the build-up of certain pests and maintain soil health.

🌻 Companion Planting

Growing selected plants together can support biodiversity and help manage some common pests.

Benefits of Organic Farming

  • Improves soil health.
  • Supports biodiversity.
  • Uses natural farming materials.
  • Helps reduce dependence on synthetic inputs.
  • Encourages environment-friendly farming practices.

Conclusion

Organic farming encourages the use of natural resources and responsible agricultural practices. It can support healthy soil, environmental protection and sustainable agriculture.
