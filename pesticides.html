What are Pesticides?

Pesticides are substances used to control pests that damage agricultural crops.

Types of Pesticides

🐛 Insecticides

These are used to control harmful insects that damage crops and plants.

🌿 Herbicides

These help manage unwanted weeds growing in agricultural fields.

🍃 Fungicides

These are used to manage fungal diseases affecting plants and crops.

Why Do Farmers Use Pesticides?

1. To protect crops from harmful pests.

2. To reduce crop damage.

3. To manage certain crop diseases and weeds.

4. To support healthy crop production when needed.

Safe Use of Pesticides

✔ Use only approved products for the intended crop and pest.

✔ Read and follow the product label instructions.

✔ Use recommended protective equipment.

✔ Keep pesticides away from children, food and drinking water.

✔ Follow the recommended waiting period before harvesting.

Effects on Environment

Improper pesticide use may affect soil quality, water sources and beneficial insects.

Conclusion

Pesticides can help protect crops when necessary, but improper use may cause harm. Farmers should follow approved recommendations.
