What is Food Safety?

Food safety means handling, cleaning and storing food properly to protect people from food-related health risks.

Cleaning Fruits and Vegetables

🍎 Wash Fruits

Wash fruits thoroughly under clean running water before eating.

🥦 Clean Vegetables

Wash vegetables properly before cutting and cooking.

🧺 Use Clean Utensils

Use clean knives, cutting boards and containers while handling food.

Awareness About Chemical Residues

Some agricultural chemicals may leave residues on fruits and vegetables. Washing produce under clean running water can help remove dirt and some surface residues. Always follow food safety practices.

Food Hygiene Tips

  • Wash your hands before handling food.
  • Keep fruits and vegetables in clean places.
  • Separate raw food from cooked food.
  • Store food at suitable temperatures.
  • Use clean drinking water.

Conclusion

Food safety is everyone's responsibility. Proper washing, cleaning, handling and storage of fruits and vegetables help maintain food hygiene and support good health.
